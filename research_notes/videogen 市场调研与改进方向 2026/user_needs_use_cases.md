## Q1. Which segments generate AI video in volume in 2026, and at what volumes?

### Takeaway
China's AI 短剧/漫剧 industry produces the most volume that can actually be measured: about 221,900 AI dramas/漫剧 went live on Douyin in H1 2026, and only about 1.3% broke even. Its production model is "抽卡" (repeated sampling), with roughly 3–10 generations per usable shot. Globally, the biggest spend sits with performance advertisers (about 2/3 of US video buyers use GenAI for creative, and over 8M Meta advertisers use its GenAI creative tools) and with e-commerce sellers. Most of that e-commerce volume, however, goes through free tools built into the platforms (TikTok Shop, 阿里妈妈 万相营造, 巨量引擎 即创) rather than BYOK API tools. L&D, game and film previs are real segments, but their volume per user is low or they are dominated by avatar tools.

### Inferences
- Measured by generations per person, Chinese AI 漫剧/短剧 studios are the heaviest batch users found anywhere: hundreds of thousands of titles, each about 100 minutes, at 3–10 samples per shot. Their workflow (script → 分镜 → per-shot generation with a sampling cap → edit) maps directly onto videogen's CSV/template + per-shot first/last frame + gallery keep/reject. Missing features block them: character/asset references, a per-shot re-roll cap, and 剪映 export.
- Performance marketers are the largest *spend* segment globally. Their per-account volume is moderate (tens of variants a month), but they need structured variation (hook × avatar × angle), naming metadata and export. Many will choose integrated ad tools (Meta Advantage+, HeyGen/Arcads/MakeUGC), so a BYOK workbench fits the agency or technical media-buyer subsegment best.
- Free platform-native tools (TikTok Shop, 万相营造, 即创) heavily serve e-commerce sellers. A BYOK local tool fits best for cross-border sellers and agencies producing many SKUs across several platforms (image-folder → video, which videogen already supports).
- Faceless channels are a shrinking and risky segment because of YouTube enforcement. Building features aimed at "mass-produced" output could carry reputational risk.
- L&D (avatar/presenter video) is dominated by Synthesia and HeyGen and is a poor fit for a clip-generation workbench. Game and film previs show low adoption and low volume.

## Q2. What are the most common pain points reported by practitioners?

### Takeaway
Across Western and Chinese sources, the most consistent complaints are: (1) credits charged for failed, stuck or moderated generations, with inconsistent refunds; (2) character/face drift across shots, ranked the top problem by 67–76% of Chinese AI-drama creators in cited surveys; (3) queues, throttling and opaque API quotas (Veo Tier-1 caps of roughly 10 requests/day, "Unlimited" plans throttled to hours); and (4) billing traps such as credit expiry, auto-renewal and non-refundable renewals. Also recurring: short-lived result URLs, watermarks tied to plan tier, provider churn (Sora API shut down on 2026-09-24), and new mandatory labeling rules in China (2025-09-01, plus the 微短剧 rules from 2026-09-01) and the EU (2026-08-02).

## Q3. What do power users explicitly ask for in batch tools?

### Takeaway
The best-evidenced asks are: (1) persistent character and scene asset libraries (reference images and look sheets) that apply across shots and episodes; (2) script → 分镜 → batch shot generation with queueing; (3) a per-shot re-roll workflow with caps; (4) handoff to editors, especially 剪映/CapCut draft export in China; (5) multi-model routing and comparison; (6) agent/MCP access, which Higgsfield, Runway and Kling all shipped in 2026; and (7) reproducibility logs plus review/approval gates for agencies. Explicit requests for a "prompt × model × seed matrix" were not found as user requests, but hosted side-by-side comparison products exist.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
Mapping to videogen's missing features, ranked by evidence strength for high-volume segments:
1. **Reference images / character sheets / asset library**: the top pain in both Chinese AI drama and Western narrative work. All four providers videogen supports (Wan, Seedance, Gemini/Veo, Kling-like) have reference-input modes. This is the most-requested capability.
2. **Per-shot re-roll and variations with a cap, plus cost-per-kept-shot reporting**: this mirrors the "抽卡师" workflow and the "cap then escalate" practice directly. It extends videogen's existing keep/reject gallery.
3. **Auto-retry on transient errors with moderation-aware rules**: retry 429/5xx/timeouts with backoff; never auto-retry policy rejections; surface failure and moderation rates per model. This addresses the most common billing complaint.
4. **Export to 剪映/CapCut drafts (and an FCPXML/EDL for Premiere/DaVinci)**: the pyJianYingDraft star count and "外部生成、剪映精修" show demand. In China this matters more than built-in concat or TTS.
5. **MCP server / CLI**: competitors made this table stakes in 2026. A local BYOK MCP server avoids the "MCP always burns credits" complaint and suits developer-creators.
6. **Side-by-side model comparison / prompt × model matrix**: hosted products exist, which validates demand, but I found no explicit user requests. It is useful as an onboarding and evaluation feature, especially given provider churn (Sora).
7. **AIGC labeling/metadata on export**: a compliance-driven need for 微短剧 (China, from 2026-09-01) and EU-facing work (from 2026-08-02).
8. **Collaboration/review**: needed by agencies, but heavy to build in a local-first tool. A lightweight shareable review export (a static HTML contact sheet with keep/reject) may be enough.
- Upscaling/frame interpolation and TTS/music/subtitles came up as "one-click assembly" features in 漫剧 platforms, but practitioners often finish in 剪映, so these rank below export.

### Gaps
- No direct Reddit or HN feature-request threads could be retrieved, so evidence for Western power-user asks comes from tool docs, vendor blogs and agency guides rather than verbatim user requests.
- I found no explicit evidence of demand for "seed matrices"; many current API models do not expose seeds consistently (not verified here).
- I found no data on how many creators export to Premiere or DaVinci versus CapCut/剪映.
